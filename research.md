## 1. NZ COALITION ONE SEAT SHORT — New Zealand / politics (hero)

- **What:** Final results of New Zealand's 7 November election, released on
  Friday 27 Nov, showed the ruling centre-right coalition with 65 seats —
  one short of a majority in the 130-seat parliament. Preliminary results
  had given it a two-seat majority; the final tally, including special
  votes, flipped it. Prime Minister Christopher Luxon now needs the support
  of the centrist Opportunity Party (7 seats) to stay in power. National
  won 40 seats, ACT 13, New Zealand First 12; Labour 36.
- **Date logic:** the election was 7 Nov, but the final count and the
  one-seat-short outcome were first reported on Friday 27 Nov.
- **Source:** Reuters, "New Zealand's ruling coalition falls just short of
  majority in final election count" —
  https://www.reuters.com/world/asia-pacific/new-zealands-ruling-coalition-falls-just-short-majority-final-election-count-2026-11-27/
- **Image wording:** "COALITION ONE SEAT SHORT" + "LUXON NEEDS OPPORTUNITY PARTY" +

  seats — one short of a majority." — the image does not claim a deal has
  been struck; it reports the count and the consequence.
